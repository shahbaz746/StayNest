📌 Business Rules
One account can manage multiple hostels.
Admin approval is required before a hostel becomes public.
Guests can browse but cannot book.
Students must sign up before booking.
Booking is confirmed only after successful payment.
Payment is processed through Stripe (initial plan).
A booked seat becomes unavailable automatically.
Other seats in the same room remain available.
Students can cancel according to the cancellation policy.
Owners cannot reject a booking after successful payment.
Maximum 5 images per hostel and 5 images per room (for MVP).
