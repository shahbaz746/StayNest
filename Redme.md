# 🚀 StayNest

> **Building the future of student accommodation, one step at a time.**

StayNest is a modern SaaS platform designed to simplify how students discover, compare, and book accommodations while empowering property owners with powerful tools to manage their listings.

This project is currently under active development and is being built using modern software engineering practices, focusing on scalability, performance, security, and user experience.

---

# 📌 Vision

To become the most trusted platform for student accommodation by connecting students with verified properties while providing property owners with a simple and powerful management solution.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to eliminate the frustration of finding and managing student accommodation by creating a platform that is:

* Fast
* Secure
* Easy to Use
* Reliable
* Scalable

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Finding a hostel or student accommodation is often frustrating.

Students usually face problems such as:

* Limited information
* No verified listings
* No real-time room availability
* Poor communication with owners
* No centralized booking platform

Property owners also struggle with:

* Managing room listings
* Tracking bookings
* Handling inquiries manually
* Maintaining occupancy records
* Reaching more students online

StayNest aims to solve these challenges through one unified platform.

---

# 💡 Solution

StayNest provides a centralized platform where:

### Students can

* Search accommodations
* View room details
* Compare prices
* Check availability
* Reserve rooms online

### Property Owners can

* Register their property
* Manage rooms
* Upload images
* Update availability
* Receive booking requests
* Manage their business efficiently
